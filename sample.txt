From the magazine:In Lieu of Flowers
By Carolyn Forché
We weren’t told as children there is no going back.
But we are allowed to keep with us a few times and places 
thin as tissue paper in the mind.
Finding these in the mind is like moving through a forest at night.
We do this alone in our rooms in the dark by light of an open door.
Then a thought comes like a bird landing on a blanket.…
